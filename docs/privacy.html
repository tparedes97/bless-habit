Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

At Bless Habit (TANIA Paredes Vasquez, Peru) we take care of what you trust us with. This explains what data we keep, why, and what control you have.

Data we keep

  • Your Google account: name, email and profile photo, to sign you in.
  • What you use in the app: your profile and onboarding answers, habits, planner, metrics, achievements, journal entries and photos.
  • Chat with Bless: the day's conversation is kept until the next day; then it is discarded and only a few notes remain ("what Bless remembers about you"), which you can see and delete in Profile.
  • Payments: if you subscribe to Premium, Paddle (web) or Google Play (Android app) processes the payment. We never see or store your card details; only a customer and subscription identifier.
  • Security: if you turn on the PIN, we only store an encrypted version (hash), never the PIN itself.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the technical data needed to send them.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: save your progress, let Bless reply in a personalized way, send you reminders and manage your subscription. We do not sell your data, and your profile, habits, journal and conversations with Bless are never used or shared for ads.

Ads in the Android app

On the free plan, the Android app shows ads from Google AdMob (a banner in Calendar and Metrics, ads you choose to watch in exchange for something —for example, an extra task or more messages with Bless— and, at most once a day, an ad after you complete all your habits). To show and measure them, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, technical device data and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example, in Europe), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it anytime in Profile → "Ad privacy". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Premium has no ads. Learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

TikTok ad measurement. We promote Bless Habit with ads on TikTok. To know whether an install came from those ads, the Android app uses the TikTok App Events SDK, which sends TikTok the device's advertising ID, technical device data (model, OS, language, IP address) and basic usage events: installing and opening the app, finishing sign-up and subscribing to Premium. We do not send your name, email, habits, diary or conversations with Bless. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Learn more in TikTok's privacy policy.

Who we share it with

With providers we need for the app to work, and only for that:

  • Google (sign-in).
  • OpenAI (generates Bless's replies from your messages and profile context).
  • Render (server) and Turso (database), where your data is hosted.
  • Paddle and Google Play (Premium payments).
  • Google AdSense (ads on the web version of the free plan) and Google AdMob (ads in the Android app's free plan).
  • TikTok (measuring Bless Habit's ads on TikTok, Android app only).

How long we keep it

For as long as you have your account. You can delete journal entries, memories and habits anytime in the app. You can delete your account and all your data in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or by email (see how to delete your account); by email, we delete your data within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep (for example, payment records, kept by Paddle).

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing ptania191@gmail.com. We will reply within a reasonable time.

Children

The app is only for people 18 and older. It is not directed to minors and we do not knowingly collect their data; if we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will let you know in the app.
